Onboarding checklist
The record has a single onboarding checklist. Each item and activity is set to apply either to all employees or only to a selected job profile.
Onboarding Phases 13 items · 7 activities
Phases are shared by checklist items and activities alike — an employee's record groups both by them. An item is simply done / not done, while an activity is scheduled and tracked. The order can be changed by dragging.
